Notable: Prompt injection attempts detected. On this day, at least 3 users sent prompts explicitly instructing the AI to erase its RemixAI identity and ignore all Web3/Solidity context. Also observed: a sophisticated multi-line system-prompt-style injection in Chinese instructing the model to "never fabricate" and adopt an analyst persona. These are coordinated attempts to jailbreak or repurpose the AI. Worth monitoring for frequency trends.

Key takeaways

  • 38% of users (285/759) engaged authentically — up from 29% on May 30. A meaningfully higher share of genuine conversational use on this day.
  • 62% of users (474/759) only clicked canned buttons and never typed anything.
  • Among authentic users, depth remains consistent — averaging 4.0 manual prompts each, covering Solidity debugging, contract development, and multi-language requests.
  • Top three canned prompts (vulnerability, explain error, explain code) account for 82.7% of all canned events — essentially unchanged from May 30.
  • Spanish-language users appeared for the first time as a distinct signal (3 users with the same exercise prompt), suggesting a Latin American user group.
  • Prompt injection activity is worth flagging — 3 users on this single day attempted to override the AI's system identity.
