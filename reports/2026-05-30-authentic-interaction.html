Key takeaways

  • 29% of users (151/515) engaged authentically — they typed their own prompts rather than clicking preset buttons. These are the users genuinely exploring the AI as a tool.
  • 71% of users (364/515) never typed a single message. They used only the canned buttons, which is still valuable product usage but does not reflect conversational AI engagement.
  • Among authentic users, engagement depth is meaningful — averaging 4.7 manual prompts each, covering real Solidity debugging, contract architecture questions, and multi-language requests.
  • The top three canned prompts (vulnerability check, explain error, explain code) account for 84% of all canned events — the button set is working well but is heavily skewed to a few high-utility actions.
  • This is one day of data. Trending this metric over time will be more meaningful than the absolute number.
